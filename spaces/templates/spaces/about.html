About StudySmart

StudySmart helps you find somewhere to study in Dublin. It shows public libraries and cafés on a map, finds the ones nearest to you, and lets you filter cafés by facilities their source data confirms.

Who it's for

University students in Dublin looking for a quiet library desk or a café to work from between lectures. Anyone else studying or working remotely in the city can use it too.

StudySmart covers the Dublin City Council area. Campuses outside it, such as UCD Belfield, will show few or no nearby results; Show nearest match still finds the closest one.

How to use it

  1. Choose a starting point. Pick your university campus, press Use my location, or press Pick on map and tap anywhere on the map.
  2. Adjust the filters. Under Filter cafés you can ask for cafés with confirmed indoor seating or Wi-Fi; these filters never hide libraries.
  3. Browse the results. The nearest matches appear as numbered cards that match the numbered map markers. Select a card or a marker to see the address, distance, facilities and where the information came from.

If nothing is close enough, use Search a wider area or Show nearest match. The layers button on the map shows area boundaries, and About the data under the results lists how many libraries and cafés each area has.

About the information

  • Libraries come from Dublin City Council open data (CC BY). Cafés come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
  • Facilities are shown only when the source records them. Unknown means the source doesn't say, not that a facility is missing. The library dataset doesn't record facilities, so library cards don't list any.
  • Seating information is not live availability: a café with indoor seating may still be full. Check opening hours before you travel.
